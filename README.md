# Globio – A1 German Readiness Check 🇩🇪

A simple assessment tool for German A1 learners to check their preparation, understand their weak areas and know what they need to practise before booking their Goethe or ÖSD exam.

## Why I Built This

Many learners complete their A1 German course and book their exam without being sure about their preparation. Some struggle to identify what they know well and where they need more practice.

While exploring this problem, I focused on a few common challenges:

- Learners are often unsure whether they are ready for the A1 exam.
- They may perform well in some areas but struggle in others.
- It can be difficult to understand their actual strengths and weaknesses.
- Getting a mock test score doesn't always explain what went wrong.
- Fear of failing and losing the exam fee makes the booking decision difficult.

## What Globio Does

Globio gives learners a short assessment to understand their current German A1 foundation.

After completing the assessment, learners can:

- See their overall score out of 100.
- Check their performance in Vocabulary, Grammar and Reading.
- Explore individual skill scores.
- Understand which areas need more practice.
- Get simple tips and learning resources to improve.

## Assessment Overview

The assessment contains 18 questions across three areas.

| Area | Questions |
|---|---:|
| Vocabulary | 6 |
| Grammar | 6 |
| Reading | 6 |
| **Total** | **18** |

### Skills Covered

- **Vocabulary:** Word Meaning, Everyday Vocabulary, Articles and Gender, Numbers and Time.
- **Grammar:** Verb Conjugation, Sentence Structure, Negation.
- **Reading:** Information Retrieval, Main Idea Comprehension, Contextual Understanding.

## Readiness Results

The prototype uses three readiness categories:

- **Strong Foundation:** Overall score of 80% or above, with every section at 80% or above.
- **Almost Ready:** Overall score of 60% or above, with every section at 60% or above.
- **Needs Practice:** Performance below these thresholds.

## Run the Project

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Open `index.html` using Live Server.
4. Start the assessment and answer all 18 questions.
5. View your readiness report and recommendations.

## Current Limitations

- The assessment currently has only 18 questions.
- Some individual skills have only one or two questions.
- The scoring thresholds are initial assumptions.
- Recommendations provide basic guidance and may not cover every learner's needs.
- Repeating the same questions may not show actual improvement.
